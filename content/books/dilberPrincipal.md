A must read book for anybody working in an office. Reading it will make you laugh a lot and make you survival in the hostile and perplexing office habitat much easier.

- Pg 97 No major religion specifically bans the pilfering of office supplies
- Pg 92 Virtual hour compensation
- Pg 100 voice mail - modern day version is online messages, great way to avoid work whilst seeming very busy
- Pg 101 I proactively found excellence in the midst of chaos
- Code: language of the future
