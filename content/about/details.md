I wanted to build a "simple" personal landing page. But simple was much harder to find then I thought. As you can tell, I got there in the end. This is my story:
<!--more-->
## The journey begins
This is my third or forth attempt. I started with trying straight html and css and immediatly ran into the various web religions. Most pages are table or should you use "div"? It just got worse from there. There seem to be more types of web tech then sand in the sea. Most seem excessivly convoluted. 
My first attempt looked nice but was hard to modify and difficult to add to. The original idea of every page is in essence a table and pages a strung together with urls, was attactive. But once you add css you are on your way to hell. Add Angular or React and the nightmare deepens. Soon I gave you. 
## The second attempt
This was another obvious one: buy a template and modify it. You can look before you buy, sounds promising. Got the template, started modifying and soon the usual un-tamable web chaos decend on my life. Yes, you can get a template and modify a bit of text, but that is it. Move or reformat anything and Good Night Charlie.
## The third attempt
I discovered static web site generators. That sounded promising, especially as I worked for companies that built dynamic web sites, that were painfully slow and walking along the edge of chaos. Not to mention the millions of dollars they spent. <br>
I like golang so it was not hard to end up with **hugo**. But here gain, they told me to use themes. Sounded resonable, but themes brought in a stack of new technologies which I was trying hard to avoid. The previous problem of trying to modify other peoples work soon brought the chaos back, which I had already experienced in the second attempt.
## Here we are
The solution came by coincident. I watch this video: ([Hugo Actually Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZFL09qhKi5I)) which finally explained the bare bones of hugo. Then AI arrived and told me to forget about themes and just use hugo pure. Suddenly building my site was a lot of fun. I just concentrate on the content and AI gets to do little css tweeks. I am still learning how to use all the features, but the entire project is refreshingly simple. You want to build a simple landing page, well, this is the way to do it.
