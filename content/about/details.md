I wanted to build a "simple" personal web site. But simple was much harder to find then I thought. As you can tell, I got there in the end. This is my story:
<!--more-->
## The journey begins
This is my third or forth attempt. To start off with I tried html and css and immediatly ran into the various web religions. Most pages are table or should you use "div"? It just got worse from there. There seem to be more types of web tech then sand in the sea. Most seem excessivly convoluted. 
My first attempt looked nice but hard to modify and difficult to add to. The original idea of every page is in essence a table and pages a strung together with urls, was attactive. But once you add css you are on your way to hell. Add Angular or React and the nightmare deepens. Soon I gave you. 
## The second attempt
Was another obvious one, buy a template and modify it. You can look before you buy, sounds promising. Got the template started modifying and soon the usual un-tamable web chaos decend on my life again. Yes, you can get a template and modify a bit of text, but that is it. Move or reformat anything and Good Night Charlie.
## The third attempt
I discovered static web site generators. That sounded promising, especially as I worked for companies that built dynamic web sites, that were painfully slow and sailing along the edge of chaos. Not to mention the millions of dollars they spent. <br>
I like golang a lot so it was not hard to end up with **hugo**. But here gain, they told me to use themes. Sounded resonable, but they brought in a stack of new technologies I was trying hard to avoid. The previous problem of trying to modify other peoples work soon brought the chaos back, which I had already experienced in the second attempt.
