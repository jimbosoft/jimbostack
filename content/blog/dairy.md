Life is short, they say. That is because we can never remember any of it. Imagine all the Friday nights you have had, all the parties you've ever been to. The people you have ever met and so on. Every Friday night I meet up with mates at the pub and every Friday it feels like it has been a big week. So I decided to write my weekly diary. If it is as boring as I think my life is I will just delete it.

## 23 Oct 26
Felt like a big week<br><br>

- My son got a place with his friends and is moving out. How fantastic, life is for living. I will pay the rent for the time being. He is moving next week. Unfortunately he lost his job because the company went under. Now he is worried and handing his CV out. Wants to be an apprentice chef. By Friday he had an offer to start as a kitchen hand with the prospect of an apprenticeship. Go team!
-  The other one is studying for his VCE. He seems to actually, finally be doing it. He is bright, but can't see the point. I get it. They are too old for school. After 15 they should start work, they can always come back to studying later. Amazingly he ask me to put a secret code in his phone to only give him 5 minutes a day with TikTok and Insta. I am impressed he came to this conclusion and I am amazed that the addiction must be so bad he has to voluntarily have me lock his phone. I guess a lot of attempts to give up and frustration must have lead to this.
- I actually had to knuckle down and work hard in order to demo my latest project during the weekly project meeting. Everybody loves a demo. Beats just talking about it. I barely understand the software and was pushed to "just get it working". I prefer to understand things first, but I had to get them off my back. Good to start a project with a lot of tech debt. This was the first project were I used "spec driven" AI development. As a software developer, that was not as much fun as agentic engineering. However the result was pretty good, but it brings a big danger of not "understanding" the software in detail.
- I also spoke to some Real Estate agents. They told me what a pain young renters are and constantly threaten to take it to the authorities if they don't get what they want. Some even hand over a list of demands before they even get the property. The picture is of a very entitled youth that can't actually do basic things like clean a bathroom or know that a fan must be used in bathrooms or mould soon forms. Made me wonder how my parenting is. Also made me wonder if we were any different. They also said that owners call they properties Taj Mahals because they are so expensive, as opposed to the older days when owners were more relaxed as properties were not that dear.
