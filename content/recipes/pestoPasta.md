## Prep
You will need 2 frying pans. One of them deep enough to mix the sauce. And a pot to boil pasta<br><br>
Dice the onion small<br>
Crush some garlic<br>
Cut off the out layer of the broccoli stems. Cut them in half and then into medium discs.<br>
Slice mushrooms, not to small<br>
Dice chicken <br>
Silver beet, remove stems and hard bits, you just want the leaves. Wash well in a sink full of cold water<br>
Now place it in a bowl and blanch. Blanching is magic and can be used in many recipes, you will hear a lot more about it here<br>
Blanch: boil water, then submerge vegetable in it. Leave it for a bit<br>
When ready to add to the bowl, chop very fine

## Cook
Fry onion in the pan, add garlic when ready. Once glassy and a bit burnt remove from pan.<br>
Pan fry the broccoli on both sides. remove and add to onion when ready<br>
Fry chicken add garlic when ready<br>
In the mean time ...<br>
In the deep frying pan, melt some butter, add and sauté mushrooms. Add a little water as to not start a fire<br>
In the mean, mean time ...<br>
Get a deep bowl, pour in as much cream as you want. Stir in pesto. Add an egg and chopped silver beet. Mix well.<br>
In the deep pan that holds the mushrooms add the sauce from the bowl you just mixed up. Heat slowly<br>
Gradually add the chicken, broccoli and onion<br>
In the mean time ...<br>
Boil some water and cook the pasta. Add a little olive oil to stop them sticking together<br>
Well done! Now serve and eat. Yummy!

## Ingredients
- chicken breast
- 1 onion
- garlic
- broccoli stems (this is your chance to use them)
- silver beet ( I use fresh from the garden, it's the best) or spinach
- mushrooms
- cream
- pesto
- 1 egg
