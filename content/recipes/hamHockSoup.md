One of the all time favourites. A heart warmer, a soup to reboot. Usually made in winter when Celeriac bulbs (root) are in season. It is the magic ingredient. Apparently only chefs know about it, but now you do too.

## Slow Cooking Time
 Many hours. At least 3, 6 are better<br>
## Ingredients

- 1 Ham Hock (Size depending on your pot)
- 200g Red lentils
- 1 celeriac bulb - Chopped into small squares
- 2 Carrots - Cut into round pieces
- 1 leek - sliced
- garlic - lots, chopped
- Nutmeg - 1 tsp
- Stock - more then 500 ml

## Process
Get a big pot. I use a le creuset. Put some oil in it and fry the leek and garlic a bit. <br>
Add the carrots. Then you are supposed to add the celeriac, but that is not realistic as it will be too much. You can try. <br>
Stir it to make it soft but not burn it <br>
Bury the ham hock in the middle and chuck the lentils and Nutmeg in <br>
Add enough stock to cover the ham hock ideally. Don't make the stock too strong or it will get to salty. The ham hock and celeriac make the flavour.<br>
simmer! Yes, simmer! Don't boil! ... Time passes, stir regularly, so it does not burn. Eventually the meat will fall of the bone, then <br>
carefully get the bone out and don't make a mess. Put it on a cutting board. Remove the bone and the skin and any fatty bits. Cut the meat, if it needs, into small pieces. Put the meat back in the pot and cook for another hour. Stir regularly.
