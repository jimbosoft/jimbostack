Dumplings Austrian style. This dish is one of my favorite childhood foods—something I've been trying to recreate for years, without much success yet, but I'm not giving up. Stay tuned for updates.

It comes from Tirol, a alpine region in Austria known for its strong sense of independence and rich culinary traditions. Tiroleans are proud people, famous not only for their resilience but also for their incredible cooking. Apparently they have a Knodel festival, I need to go there.

 <!--more-->

- 250 g Dumpling bread
- Salt
- 1/4 l (1 cup) Milk
- 1 medium onion
- 1/4 cup of all-purpose flour
- 2 Piece Eggs
- 3 EL Breadcrumbs
- 80 g Bacon, very finely diced
- 30 g Butter
- 2 tbl fresh parsley
- Chives
- Salt water
